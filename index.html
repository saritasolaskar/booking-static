Pricing Transparency

What's Included?

Clear guidelines on base inclusions and route-dependent charges to prevent surprises and avoid pricing disputes.

Standard Base Inclusions

  • Vehicle: Clean, well-maintained vehicle in the booked category.
  • Driver: Experienced, verified commercial highway chauffeur.
  • Fuel: Included for agreed route kilometers and itinerary.
  • Route-based travel arrangements: Coordinated pickups and drop-offs.
  • Customer support: Dedicated coordination assistance during travel.

Route & Quotation Dependent Items

Tolls & Highway Taxes: Fastag charges vary by highway corridor.
State Border Permits: Commercial entry taxes when crossing state lines.
Parking Charges: Settled based on actual receipt where applicable.
Driver Allowance: Applicable for overnight and multi-day travel.
All applicable inclusions and charges will be communicated in the quotation before confirmation.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking one-way, round-trip, and outstation cabs with TransitFleets.

Do you provide one-way outstation cabs?
Yes. One-way outstation travel can be arranged based on the pickup location, destination, date and vehicle availability.
Do you provide round-trip cabs?
Yes. Round-trip options are available for customers who require transportation for both onward and return journeys.
Can I book a cab from any city in India?
TransitFleets serves intercity and long-distance travel requirements across India, subject to route, date and vehicle availability.
Which vehicles can I book?
Vehicle options may include sedans, SUVs, MUVs, premium vehicles and group travel vehicles, depending on the location and requirement.
Can I book a cab for multiple days?
Yes. Multi-day vehicle and driver arrangements can be requested for suitable travel itineraries.
Are tolls and parking included?
Applicable tolls, parking, permits, driver allowances and other charges depend on the route and quotation. The applicable inclusions will be communicated before confirmation.
Can I request a specific vehicle?
You can request a preferred vehicle. Availability depends on the pickup location and travel date.
How do I get an outstation cab quotation?
Submit your trip details through the enquiry form or contact TransitFleets through WhatsApp or phone (+91 98810 43606). Our team can then provide available vehicle options and the applicable quotation.
